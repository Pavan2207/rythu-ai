What to verify

Six details worth checking

  • Scheme or policy name and the issuing office.
  • Last date to apply or report a loss.
  • Who is eligible and any land-size limits.
  • Documents required: land record, Aadhaar, bank passbook.
  • Where to submit: office, portal or bank branch.
  • Premium, subsidy amount and payment timeline.
