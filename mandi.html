Hold or sell?

Questions to ask before deciding

  • Compare today's modal price against your cost of production, not just yesterday's rate.
  • Check storage cost, moisture limits and whether your stock can wait safely.
  • Ask your APMC for arrivals next week and any local procurement date.
  • This prototype does not generate a hold or sell recommendation.

Before you sell

Verify these with the mandi

  • Grade, moisture and bag weight accepted by the buyer.
  • Commission, weighing and loading charges.
  • Payment date and receipt details.
  • The current official rate on the APMC board.
