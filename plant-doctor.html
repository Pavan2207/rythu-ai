How this page works

Speech in, guidance out

1 · SpeakTap Start voice command and describe the crop, age, symptom and affected area. Clips stop automatically after 28 seconds.
2 · TranscribePrisma v3.0 detects the spoken language, or you can lock one. Code-mixed language pairs need v3.0.
3 · AnswerCautious rule-based answers are translated by the server into your selected text and voice languages. With local Evon configured, Evon writes the answer directly instead.
4 · ListenTimbre reads the translated answer in the selected voice language. Every language is spoken by the API, not the browser.
  • No pesticide dose is prescribed. Confirm the cause with your KVK before spraying.
  • Attached plant photos are not analysed or uploaded.
